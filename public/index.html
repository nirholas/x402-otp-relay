x402-otp-relay

The 'code sent to your email' step, solved for agents. Rent a disposable relay mailbox, hand its address to whatever needs to email you a code, then poll for the extracted OTP. Real inbound SMTP; codes are ranked with confidence scores and surrounding context. Pay in USDC on Base or Solana; the client picks the rail.

Paid with HTTP 402 USDC on Base or Solana Your client picks the rail

Call it

curl -i -X POST http://localhost:4035/mailbox \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{}'

An unpaid request returns 402 with an accepts array holding both rails, each carrying outputSchema.input / outputSchema.output — everything an agent needs to pay and then call the route correctly.
